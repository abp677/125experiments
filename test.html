Collect Yourself

10 min

Take a quiet moment to orient. Write or type (3–5 sentences):

⚙️

Pick a Track

20 min

Choose the track that fits your situation:

Track A: Generating Questions

For you if you have < 10 questions or your topic still feels fuzzy

  • ➤ Revisit your favorite topic
  • ➤ Use "Go Small with Questions"
  • ➤ Add 5 new specific, factual questions
  • ➤ Optional: test one in Google Scholar or Gemini

Track B: Expanding Sources

For you if you have 10+ questions but < 10 sources

  • ➤ Run 3–5 of your best questions as searches
  • ➤ Apply 20/80 rule (20% scan / 80% notice reaction)
  • ➤ Add new sources + 1-word reaction to your log

Track C: Refining Focus

For you if you have 10+ sources and want to narrow

  • ➤ Pick 3 "meh" sources
  • ➤ Answer: Why didn't it grab me?
  • ➤ Write "I'm more interested in ___ than ___"
  • ➤ Use insight to refine/cluster questions
🕐 Checkpoint: Write one sentence: "What changed or clicked for me during this block?"
💬

Soundboarding Pairs

12–15 min

Find a partner (not your usual one):

1️⃣ Share your topic + one question that still feels alive

2️⃣ Say what's interesting and what you're unsure about

3️⃣ Listener responds: "What's intriguing? What's unclear? Where could this live as a discipline?"

🧩

Mini-Clustering

10 min

1️⃣ Highlight 3–4 questions that connect

2️⃣ Give each cluster a working name (e.g., AI & Health Bias, AI & Sports Metrics)

3️⃣ Add one follow-up question under each cluster
